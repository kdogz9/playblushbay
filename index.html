Heal the Ocean, One Bubble at a Time

In Blush Bay, you run a rehabilitation center for sea creatures. From clicking bubbles in mini-games to rescue stray fish, to configuring delicate water stats in your custom tanks, every choice helps nurse your patients back to health. Once they're strong enough, release them back into the wild to make room for more rescues!

Aquarium Management Loops

🫧

Bubble Mini-Games

Spot hurt sea life out in the wild! Pop the rescue bubbles in fast-paced mini-games to carefully secure them and bring them back to safety.

🧪

Lab-Grown Nutrition

Cultivate specialized ingredients inside your research lab. Feed them into your processing machines to manufacture custom diets for sick fish.

🎀

Decorate & Expand

Earn sweet donations from impressed aquarium visitors. Spend your funds on buying premium tanks, pastel furniture, and cute aesthetic props.

The Healing Cycle

🐠 Stat Management
Step 01

Stabilize Your Patient

Every creature rescued has distinct medical stats. Keep their tanks perfectly configured to their biological needs. Trigger healing mini-games to directly treat wounds and infections.

🤝 Donation Economy
Step 02

Earn Visitor Donations

As your aquarium gets healthier and prettier, guests will explore your facilities. Happy visitors drop donations based on the overall quality, decoration level, and medical standards of your sanctuary.
